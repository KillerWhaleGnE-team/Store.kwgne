About this software

A free tool for capturing screenshots and screen recordings on Windows. You can pick a region, a window or the whole screen, annotate the result, and send it to a number of places automatically.

Credit and license

ShareX is developed by its team and contributors under the GPL. Download it from the official site or its GitHub releases. Platform availability changes over time, so check the official site for the builds that are current.
