About this software

Luanti, formerly known as Minetest, is a free, open-source voxel game engine with easy modding and game creation. It has a built-in content browser (ContentDB) where you can download games and mods without leaving the app.

Credit and license

Luanti's engine is under the LGPL, and most of its bundled media is under CC BY-SA 3.0, which asks for credit and sharing under the same terms. Games and mods you add from ContentDB each carry their own licenses. Platform availability changes over time, so check the official site for the builds that are current.
