About this software

A free 3D kart racer that cares more about fun than realism. The playable characters are mascots of open-source projects. Race against computer opponents on your own, or play with up to four friends on one PC.

Credit and license

SuperTuxKart is developed by its community. Its code is under the GPL, and its art, sound and music come from many contributors under several free licenses, with credit to each. Please download it from the official site so you get the version they maintain. Platform availability changes over time, so check the official site for the builds that are current.
